# SOFT Snakes and Ladders: Game Rules

**Safe Online Future for Teens.** A board game that teaches safe online habits. For 2 players, ages 8 to 16.

## What you need

One phone, tablet, or computer. Two players share it and take turns. You can also play against the computer.

## Getting ready

1. Tap **Play**.
2. Choose **2 Players** (play with a friend) or **Player vs Computer**.
3. Each player picks a character and types a name. If you skip the name, the game uses "Player 1", "Player 2", or "Computer".
4. Tap **Start game**. Both characters start on square 1, "Online".

## Taking a turn

1. Tap the big die (or press the Space bar on a computer).
2. Your character hops forward one square at a time, the same number of squares as the die shows.
3. Then it is the next player's turn.

## Ladders and trains: safe habits

Some squares show a **safe online habit**, like "Always check if online information is correct." A ladder or a train track starts on these squares.

If you land there, you **climb the ladder or ride the train** up the board, and a short message praises the safe habit.

| Start square | Goes up to | Safe habit on the square |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 11 | My family knows which websites and apps I use. |
| 6 | 7 | Always check if online information is correct. |
| 8 (train) | 15 | Only open safe messages. If in doubt, ask your elders. |
| 14 | 24 | I know that online content has copyright. |
| 18 | 19 | I know people online are strangers and are not always truthful. |
| 20 (train) | 22 | If someone online asks to meet, talk to your elders first. |
| 23 | 28 | Never be rude to anyone online. |

## Snakes: risky habits

Some squares show a **risky online habit**, like "Sharing your photos publicly." A snake's head sits on these squares.

If you land on a snake's head, you **slide down the snake** to its tail. A message card then shows a real life situation and a **Safe Tip**. Read it together, then tap **I understand**.

| Snake head | Slides down to | Risky habit on the square |
|---|---|---|
| 10 (green snake) | 5 | Sharing and forwarding unverified information. |
| 25 (pink snake) | 12 | Sharing your photos publicly. |
| 29 (yellow snake) | 14 on Board A, 13 on Board B | Sharing personal information with online strangers. |

There are 60 different snake messages. You will not see the same one again until you have seen them all.

## Special rules

- **Roll a 6, roll again.** A 6 gives you an extra turn. After three extra turns in a row, the turn passes to the next player.
- **Exact roll to finish.** You must land exactly on square 30. If your roll is too big, you stay where you are and the game tells you the number you need.

These two rules can be switched off in **Settings**.

## Winning

The first player to reach square 30 becomes a **Digital Safety Champion!** The win screen shows the safety lessons that player met during the game.

## Good to know

- **Pause:** tap the pause button for Resume, Restart, Rules, Settings, and Quit.
- **Language:** choose English, Urdu, or Both in Settings.
- **Sound:** sound effects and music can be turned on or off separately.
- **Saved games:** if the game is closed by accident, it is saved. Tap **Continue game** next time.
- **Private:** the game works without internet, has no ads, no accounts, and collects no information.
