## Full description: English

Climb the ladders of good online habits and slide down the snakes of risky ones!

SOFT Snakes and Ladders is a friendly board game for children aged 8 to 16, made by SOFT: Safe Online Future for Teens. Every ladder starts on a safe online habit, like checking if information is true. Every snake sits on a risky habit, like sharing personal information with strangers.

When a player lands on a snake, a friendly card shows a real life situation from the online world, with a simple Safe Tip. Topics include:

• Keeping personal information private
• Strong passwords and secret codes
• Spotting strangers and red flags
• Kindness and stopping cyberbullying
• Checking news before forwarding
• Avoiding scam calls and fake prize messages
• Photos and your digital footprint
• Safe gaming chats and in game purchases
• Healthy screen time
• Respecting copyright
• Blocking, reporting, and always telling a trusted adult

FEATURES
• 2 players on one device, or play against the computer
• 8 original cartoon characters to choose from
• 60 safety messages that never repeat until all are seen
• English, Urdu, or both languages together
• Gentle music and sound effects, each with its own switch
• Win the title of Digital Safety Champion and see the lessons you met

MADE FOR FAMILIES AND SCHOOLS
• No ads
• No in app purchases
• No accounts or logins
• No data collection of any kind
• Works completely offline

Great for classrooms, family time, and digital literacy workshops.
